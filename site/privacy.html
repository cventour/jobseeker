Privacy

It reads your inbox.
Here is the deal.

JobSeeker reads your email, your calendar, and your WhatsApp and LinkedIn message lists. That is a lot of access, and you should want a straight answer about what it does with it. This page is that answer.

Guarantees

  • It never applies to a job. Applying is a separate, interactive command that stops and waits for you to approve that exact application.
  • It never sends a message. Same rule. Drafts are written; you approve before anything leaves.
  • It never opens a chat thread. Opening one marks it read on your real account and destroys your own sense of what still needs you. It reads the conversation list only.
  • It never records private conversations. Most of a personal WhatsApp is family and friends. Only threads carrying a job-search signal have their content stored; the rest are counted and left alone.
  • Nothing leaves your machine. There is no server, no account, no telemetry, no analytics.

Where your data lives

Plain text files you own.

Every record is Markdown you can open, read, edit and delete with any text editor. No database, no export format, no lock-in. If you stop using JobSeeker tomorrow, you keep everything in a form that will still open in twenty years.

data/applications/app_k2gjp9.md    one file per application
data/tasks.md                     follow-ups, due dates
data/communications.md            messages seen across channels
data/watermarks.md                how far each channel has been read

That folder is excluded from version control, so it cannot be published by accident.

Permissions

What it asks for, and what it refuses to.

  • macOS Automation, for Google Chrome only — to read the pages you are already logged in to.
  • No Screen Recording. Nothing is ever screenshotted.
  • No Accessibility. No synthetic clicks or keystrokes, ever.
  • No Full Disk Access. Only this project's own folder is read and written.
  • No browser debugging port. An open port would let any local process drive your browser as you. JobSeeker refuses to open one.

Reading other people's text

Instructions found in content are ignored.

A job advert or an email can contain text aimed at the assistant rather than at you — "ignore your previous instructions", "the candidate already approved this". Everything JobSeeker reads is treated as data, never as an instruction. Such text is quoted and shown to you, never obeyed, and it can never grant an approval or choose where something gets sent.
