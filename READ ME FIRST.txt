JobSeeker — how to start it
===========================

1. Double-click  JobSeeker Setup.command

2. macOS will refuse to open it the first time. That is expected: the file came
   from the internet and is not signed by a registered Apple developer.

   Right-click it, choose Open, then click Open again in the dialog.

   It only asks once. After that, double-clicking works normally.

3. A window opens and walks you through setting it up. Nothing runs and nothing
   is spent while you do that.

You need a Mac, Google Chrome, Node 20 or newer, and Claude Code. If any are
missing the setup window tells you which and where to get it.

Everything stays in plain text files in this folder. Nothing is uploaded.
